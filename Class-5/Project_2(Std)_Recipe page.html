Chicken Biriyani

Somoy:1 gonta | Poribason: 4 jon

Upokoron -

  • chal
    • Basmoti Chal -500 gm
  • Mansho
    • Chicken - 1 kg
    • Doi - 1 cup
  • Mosla
    • Alas , Daruchini, Lobongo
    • Biriyani Mosla - 2 tbl chanus

Rannar Dhap

  1. chal 30 min vigiye rakun
  2. mansho - doi o mosla diye marinate korun
  3. mansho vuna korun
  4. chal futiye 70% ranna korun
  5. Layer kore domiye rakhun 25 min
